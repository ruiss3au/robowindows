# Code of conduct

RoboWindows contributors and participants must keep project spaces respectful,
welcoming, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate exposure
of private information, and sustained personal attacks are unacceptable. Give
technical criticism clearly and receive it in good faith. Respect the legal and
privacy boundaries around guest software, test media, and device evidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the project or its community. Report conduct concerns through
the private channel described in `SECURITY.md`; do not publish sensitive details.
